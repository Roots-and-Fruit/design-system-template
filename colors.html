Primary ramp

Five steps for charts, illustrations, and progressive emphasis. Use it deliberately, not as general decoration, and keep chart series far enough apart to stay legible at small sizes.

--cds-primary-1 … --cds-primary-5

Pairing rules

Contrast is a gate, not a preference Body text needs WCAG AA (4.5:1). Display sizes may use AA Large (3:1). Text on a filled surface uses its paired on-* token, which means dark ink on the accent fill, never white.
Fill Text token Sample
primary on-primary Sign in
accent on-accent Book a call
surface-dark on-dark Callout title
primary-soft primary-deep Selected
