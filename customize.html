Make it your own

This kit ships as Example Brand on purpose. The fastest way to replace it is to hand the checklist to an agent and answer a few short questions.

Start here

Simply tell your Agent:

Walk me through this project's customization checklist

The agent should open CUSTOMIZE.md and AGENTS.md (Job B), interview you for logos, colours, and type, then apply brand.json with npm run customize.

What you will be asked

# Topic You bring
1 Logos Files into assets/ and assets/logos/
2 Name and feel Brand name plus one or two sentences of voice
3 Colours An existing palette, or a vibe and one must-use colour
4 Fonts Brand type, or permission to keep / pick open fonts
5 Shape (optional) Pill vs square buttons; serif vs sans headings
6 Apply Agent runs customize and rewrites DESIGN.md prose

Full wording for agents lives in CUSTOMIZE.md next to brand.json in this repo.
