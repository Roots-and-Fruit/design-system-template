For partners and press

Media Kit

For sponsors, partners, and anyone adding Example Brand to a website blurb or event page. Download a logo, follow the short rules, and paste the copy if you need a description.

Logo pack

These three wordmarks cover almost every partner placement. Prefer SVG on the web; use PNG when a host requires raster. Replace the placeholder files under assets/logos/ when you customise the kit.

Usage

Four common mistakes, each with a good example and a bad one. If it looks like the bad side, do not ship it.

Clear space

Good

Leave room around the mark so it can breathe.

Bad
Partner Co

Do not crowd the logo with other names or edges.

Proportions

Good

Scale evenly. Keep the natural wordmark shape.

Bad

Do not stretch, squash, rotate, or add effects.

Contrast

Good

Flat white mark on a dark field.

Bad

Do not put a light mark on a light background.

Do not invent a mark

Good

Use the official wordmark as provided.

Bad
Example Brand

Do not retype the name or recolor the mark.

Permission & contact

You may use these logos to identify Example Brand as a partner, sponsor, speaker, or featured brand, as long as the usage rules above are followed and the mark is not altered. For anything else, email hello@example.com.

Customising this kit? Drop your real marks into assets/logos/, fill the Media Kit fields in brand.json, and run npm run customize.
